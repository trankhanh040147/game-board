The original downloaded GLB (Knight.glb) is not shipped in this repository; it

Modifications made by this project
----------------------------------
Only the animation data was used. The Knight's meshes, materials, textures and
skeleton are NOT shipped or loaded at runtime. Animations were sampled offline
and retargeted onto Blade & Mount's own procedural fighter rig; the resulting
baked clip data (public/assets/knight-anim.json) is therefore also CC0.
